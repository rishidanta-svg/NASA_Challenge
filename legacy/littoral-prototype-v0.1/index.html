WORKSPACE GUIDE

Explore the coast, inspect the evidence.

Select a layer or acquisition in the left panel. Click a transect on the map to update its profile. Drag to pan and use + / − to zoom.

Enable comparison to swipe between illustrative June and October views. The Analyze workspace lets you test a cross-shore offset.

Use Evidence to download the profile template and import your own profiles. Synthetic map features are never replaced by an imported profile alone.

All initial visualisations use demonstration data.
