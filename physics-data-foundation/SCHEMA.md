# Dataset schema

The comparison grid is EPSG:32618, north up, 29 columns × 52 rows, with 10 m spacing and upper-left corner at E 432250 m, N 4005100 m. Zero-based cell centers use `x = 432255 + 10 * column` and `y = 4005095 - 10 * row`. The footprint uses the handoff's WGS84 box and the FRF alongshore range 700–1190 m. `study_area_fraction` is the fraction of each cell intersecting that footprint. There are 1,450 intersecting cells. The verified NISAR source-center mask leaves 1,310 eligible centers per polarization and date.

## Acquisition data

- `observation` indexes sensor acquisitions. `time` holds decoded UTC. `id`, `sensor`, `group`, `reference_id`, `reference_utc` and `offset_from_reference_seconds` are one-dimensional fields. Positive offsets are later than the reference. The June and October reference times are the corresponding NISAR CMR acquisition interval midpoint. Additional acquisitions use the closer NISAR midpoint as a reference.
- `x` and `y` are easting and northing cell centers in meters. `longitude` and `latitude` are WGS84 coordinates at those same centers. The affine and WKT are in `spatial_ref`.
- Raster values use float32 with NaN for unavailable data. Each observation occupies only its own variables. Masks present on an acquisition use 1 for true and 0 for false within their defined footprint. NaN denotes an unavailable mask or an undefined cell. Original categorical codes are separate bands.
- `variable_dictionary.json` enumerates every spatial variable with its unit, description and source posting. The comparison NetCDF and GeoTIFF carry their own change variable descriptions.

| Variable family | Physical quantity and support |
|---|---|
| `nisar_HHHH`, `nisar_HVHV` | NISAR Frequency A GCOV gamma-zero power, dimensionless. Ancillary subswath, exception, looks, gamma-to-sigma factors and original quality masks are included. Native grid posting is 10 m. |
| `nisar_*_db`, `s1_*_db` | Radar backscatter in dB, computed as 10 log10 of strictly positive measured power. |
| `s1_VV_gamma0`, `s1_VH_gamma0` | Catalyst/Planetary Computer RTC gamma-zero power. Pixels are posted at 10 m and catalogued IW GRDH range and azimuth resolutions are approximately 20 and 22 m. Scene incidence near/far angles are from RTC GeoTIFF tags. The catalogue lacks a measured local incidence-angle raster. |
| `s2_B02/B03/B04/B08_reflectance` | Cloud-screened BOA reflectance, unitless. Native bands are 10 m. |
| `s2_B11/B12_reflectance` | Cloud-screened BOA reflectance, unitless. Native bands are 20 m. Four 10 m cells may carry the same 20 m observation. |
| `s2_ndvi`, `s2_ndwi`, `s2_mndwi` | `(B08−B04)/(B08+B04)`, `(B03−B08)/(B03+B08)`, and `(B03−B11)/(B03+B11)`. Indices require clear valid nonnegative inputs and a positive sum above 1e-6. MNDWI uses area-averaged green over each native 20 m SWIR cell with full green support. |

Original 1 m FRF rasters, 1 m UTM display derivatives and transects remain in the source handoff. |

Sentinel-2 raw data accounting uses the producer XML. `BOA = (producer DN + BOA_ADD_OFFSET) / BOA_QUANTIFICATION_VALUE`, with producer DN 0 masked before conversion. The matching Earth Search COG encodings are checked on mutually positive samples. The native windows retain the producer's original DNs, original grid geometry and scene classification. The cloud-screened raster keeps valid signed BOA values. Scene incidence bounds, tile-wide cloud proportion and site cloud proportion have different spatial support.

The `*_change_valid` bands are 1 where October-minus-June is defined on joint support. `all_sensor_valid_overlap` includes all six optical bands, both Sentinel‑1 and both NISAR polarizations, and LiDAR joint support. LiDAR change uses the upstream paired native cells, then area aggregation. Separate June and October elevation means can summarize different underlying cells. Terrain change is in meters. Radar change is in dB. Reflectance and index changes are unitless.

## Environmental records

`observations.parquet` is a long station/variable time series. It includes `timestamp_utc`, `station_id`, `variable`, `value`, `unit`, `feature_eligible`, `source_quality_json`, `source_artifact`, `station_latitude`, `station_longitude`, `vertical_datum`, and `source_priority`. NDBC archival and preliminary records remain in the raw and tabular observations. If both are eligible at the same instant, the archival value takes precedence in features.

- NOAA CO-OPS 8651370 supplies 6-minute observed and predicted height in meters MLLW and 6-minute wind speed, direction and gust. Observed water levels marked verified or preliminary are retained with source flags. The features admit only rows with zero error flags and keep the sample standard deviation separately. Tidal stage uses the slope between the surrounding 6-minute predicted heights. Rising and falling are outside ±0.01 m/h. The observed-minus-predicted residual uses an observed height and a prediction at the exact same station timestamp.
- USACE 17 m and 26 m Waveriders provide significant wave height, peak and mean periods, and directional measurements on their own 30-minute time bases. Wave angles are meteorological degrees from true north. FRF archive energy and direction QC must equal 1 for the respective features. NDBC 44056 republishes observations from the FRF 17 m buoy. Treat NDBC 44056 and that FRF record as overlapping provenance. DUKN7 wind is NOAA alternate delivery from the Duck site.
- FRF rain provides 10-minute measured precipitation accumulations in mm, with source sensor and QC codes. Only QC 1 enters features. The source labels each bin with a sample time and does not identify its boundary convention. Rain summaries keep labels at least 10 minutes from both requested window boundaries, require every expected 10-minute slot in that guarded interval, and sum only those observed bins. A matched 0 mm is a measured zero and a missing total is null.
- FRF runup is the 2% exceedance elevation of total water level along the instrument transect in meters NAVD88 (geoid12b). It includes tide, surge, setup and runup. QC 1 or 2 is required. The bulk hydrodynamics series has the exact archive timestamp and measured cross-shore coordinates. Data files keep every tenth cross-shore sample from the source, approximately 1 m apart. Features use the nearest retained x to 80, 120 and 190 m FRF and record actual x/y in `source_quality_json`. QC 1 and a valid numeric measurement are required.
- CO-OPS MLLW heights, terrain NAVD88 and FRF hydrodynamic NAVD88 elevations retain their original vertical datums. Any later datum conversion requires a geographically and vertically appropriate transformation.
- Direct beach moisture, roughness and vegetation structure are marked unavailable in the manifest. Rain, SCL and backscatter remain independently measured variables and classifications.

`nearest.parquet` uses acquisition `id` plus `station_id` and `variable` as its key. It returns the closest quality-passing sample inside the stated symmetric tolerance, with the earlier timestamp winning exact ties. Tolerances are 12 min for 6-minute NOAA records, 35 min for waves, 15 min for rain, and 30 min for hourly runup and nearshore hydrodynamics. A positive offset may be a later observation.

`antecedent.parquet` uses the same key plus `window_hours`. The nominal windows are 1, 6, 24 and 72 hours, plus 168 for rain. Point windows are open on the left and closed at the acquisition. Non-rain summaries require at least 75% of nominal samples and include count, coverage, range, and mean as applicable. Directions use the circular mean and concentration, with mean omitted below 0.05 concentration. Rain uses the guarded and complete-bin rule above. `cross_date.parquet` reports the signed change for the same station/variable at each sensor pair with both observation UTCs and offsets. Direction changes are wrapped to [−180°, 180°).

Environmental observations retain the actual timestamps and station locations. Distinct stations, source products and temporal tolerances remain available in the tables.
